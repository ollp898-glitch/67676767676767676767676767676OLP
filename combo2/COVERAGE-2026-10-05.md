## Changes

- Strip the exact Player Props suffix for supported team sports, so NFL prop rows refer to the same game.
- Reviewed league-scoped aliases for soccer, NHL and cricket; retain exact opponents, sport, competition and five-minute UTC time check.
- Fourteen reviewed tennis doubles event identities with both source and provider event IDs, all four player labels and participant IDs. No fuzzy or generic surname matching. New unreviewed rosters remain unmatched.
- Use Flashscore's real event-ID route for doubles. Combining two participant hashes into an encoded team slug produced HTTP 404; the event-ID route is also used by the live Flashscore page.
- Native NHL overtime winner/totals/spreads, NFL first-quarter totals/spreads and singles tennis games handicap. Other periods, different lines, incomplete markets and game/set metrics cannot be substituted. First-quarter totals in this captured Combo set have no matching offered line; support does not manufacture a quote.
- Every unmatched event is recorded in paged Combo 2.0 diagnostics with its source IDs, facts and rejection reason.

No ordinary Scanner, Combo selection policy, fair-probability calculation or workflow schedule changes. Odds collection remains once per scanner snapshot with two seconds between completed event requests. Existing same-snapshot caches are retained; new matching is applied on the next scanner snapshot.

## Remaining gaps

Full event coverage is **not achieved**: 108/202 groups remain unmatched (104 tennis, 4 cricket). 17 tennis groups have matching identities but start times differing by more than five minutes; three have a qualification/main-draw conflict. The other 84 tennis groups include unsupported rosters, full-name differences and events not present in captured sport/day feeds.

The M15 Pontevedra tournament was also checked through Flashscore search and its tournament page; its embedded summary-fixtures feed contained zero upcoming events. This establishes a concrete source gap for that check, not proof that every missing match is permanently unavailable.
